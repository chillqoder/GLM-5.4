02 — How it works

From rough to running
in six weeks.

No discovery phase that outlives your enthusiasm. Here is the whole process, loop by loop.

  1. Step 01 · Day 1

    Listen

    A 45-minute call. You explain the idea in your own words; we ask the awkward questions. By the next day you have a one-page plan and a fixed price.

  2. Step 02 · Week 1

    Sketch

    Clickable screens before any real code. You’ll hate some of it. That’s the point: it’s cheap to change now.

  3. Step 03 · Weeks 2–5

    Build

    One-week sprints. AI writes the first draft of everything; we review, refactor and delete most of it. Every Friday you get a working link.

  4. Step 04 · Week 6

    Ship

    Launch, handover, and documentation your future self will thank you for. Then we stay on call.

The models type.

First drafts, boilerplate, tests, migrations. The parts nobody remembers writing.

We think.

Architecture, taste, scope, the awkward questions. The last 10% that takes 50% of the time.

You decide.

Feedback every Friday. Yes or no. And the moment it goes live.
